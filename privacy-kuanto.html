Kuanto Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary: Kuanto has no sign-up, collects no personal data, shows no ads and includes no analytics. Your progress is stored only on your device. The GeoGuess game loads a Google map, and to do so your device connects to Google's servers.

This policy describes how Kuanto, the estimation game by Qinvix for iOS and Android, handles information.

1. Data controller

Qinvix is an independent mobile app development project.
Contact: hola@qinvix.com

2. Data we collect

Qinvix does not collect personal data through Kuanto. The app has no user accounts or sign-up, and we have no servers that receive your data.

Your progress (scores, XP, level, streaks, stats and settings such as language or dark mode) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to Qinvix or to third parties.

3. Third-party services

Kuanto uses two Google services that need an internet connection. When they are used, your device communicates directly with Google, which receives the technical data involved in any connection (such as IP address and basic device and app information):

  • Google Maps: displays the map in the GeoGuess game. Kuanto does not request or use your location.
  • Google Fonts: downloads the app's typeface.

Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Every question includes a link to the source of the fact. If you open it, you leave the app for an external website with its own privacy policy.

4. Advertising and analytics

Kuanto shows no ads and includes no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

5. App store data

When you download Kuanto from Apple's App Store or Google Play, those platforms may collect data under their own policies. That processing is carried out directly by Apple or Google.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Children

Kuanto is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data from any user.

7. Your rights and how to delete your data

Because Qinvix holds no data about you, there is nothing we can look up, correct or delete on your behalf. You can erase your progress at any time from Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

To exercise your rights over data processed by Google, contact Google.

8. Changes to this policy

If Kuanto adds features that involve processing personal data (for example, accounts or online leaderboards), we will update this policy before releasing them. The date of the latest revision appears at the top of this document.

9. Contact

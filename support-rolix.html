Rolix Support

Last updated: October 2026

Need help? Write to hola@qinvix.com and include your device model, OS version and app version (Settings → About). We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rolix free?
Yes. Rolix is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Where is my progress stored? Can I move it to a new phone?
Progress is stored only on your device (Rolix has no accounts or servers), so it is not transferred automatically to another device. If your platform restores app data from a device backup, it may be restored with it.

How do I reset my progress?
Settings → Reset progress. Uninstalling the app also erases it.

I'm stuck on a level. Can I see the solution?
Yes, every puzzle has a "Show solution" button.

Which languages are supported?
English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian and Japanese.

I found a bug or have a suggestion.
Please email us with the game and level number and a short description. It helps a lot.

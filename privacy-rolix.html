Rolix Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary: Rolix has no sign-up, collects no personal data, shows no ads, has no in-app purchases and includes no analytics. Your progress is stored only on your device. The app downloads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your device connects to Google's servers.

This policy describes how Rolix, the logic puzzle collection by Qinvix for iOS and Android, handles information.

1. Data controller

Qinvix is an independent mobile app development project.
Contact: hola@qinvix.com

2. Data we collect

Qinvix does not collect personal data through Rolix. The app has no user accounts or sign-up, and we have no servers that receive your data.

Your progress (completed levels, stars, streaks, statistics and settings such as language, sound or vibration) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to Qinvix or to third parties.

3. Third-party services

Rolix uses one Google service that needs an internet connection:

  • Google Fonts: downloads the app's typeface. Your device communicates directly with Google, which receives the technical data involved in any connection (such as IP address and basic device and app information).

Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

If you open the Qinvix website or use the share function from Settings, you leave the app for an external website or app with its own privacy policy.

4. Advertising, purchases and analytics

Rolix shows no ads, offers no in-app purchases and includes no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. It does not request or use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

5. App store data

When you download Rolix from Apple's App Store or Google Play, those platforms may collect data under their own policies. That processing is carried out directly by Apple or Google.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Children

Rolix is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data from any user.

7. Your rights and how to delete your data

Because Qinvix holds no data about you, there is nothing we can look up, correct or delete on your behalf. You can erase your progress at any time from Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

To exercise your rights over data processed by Google, contact Google.

8. Changes to this policy

If Rolix adds features that involve processing personal data (for example, ads, accounts or online leaderboards), we will update this policy before releasing them. The date of the latest revision appears at the top of this document.

9. Contact
